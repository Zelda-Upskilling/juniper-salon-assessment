How offers work
  1. 1You add an openingService, stylist and appointment time.
  2. 2We offer it in signup orderOne matching client gets time to reply.
  3. 3You finish in SquareWhen they accept, confirm or release the hold.

We only contact clients whose service, availability and stylist preference fit. Each gets 15 minutes to reply. Acceptance holds the spot until you finish the booking in Square. The quick demo uses 20 seconds.

Offers go out between 9 am and 7 pm, leaving at least 30 minutes to reach the salon.
